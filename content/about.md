## Who We Are

GOOUTO (Yiwu Gongbei Trading Co., Ltd.) is a B2B wholesale showroom based in Yiwu, China — the world's largest small commodity trading hub. We specialize in outdoor products, outdoor sports equipment, outdoor toys, and holiday party supplies, serving importers, distributors, Amazon sellers, supermarket buyers, and gift wholesalers worldwide.

With over **1,000+ products** across **19 categories**, we offer factory-direct pricing, flexible MOQ, and comprehensive OEM/ODM services. Our mission is to make sourcing from China simple, transparent, and profitable for B2B buyers globally.

## Why Source from GOOUTO?

**Factory Direct Prices** — We work directly with manufacturers in Yiwu and surrounding areas, cutting out middleman markups. You get the same prices as if you visited the factory yourself.

**Flexible MOQ** — Most products have MOQ starting from 50-100 pcs. We support mixed orders across different products and colors within the same category.

**Quality Assurance** — Every order goes through pre-shipment inspection. We offer CE, EN71, FDA certified products and provide test reports on request.

**OEM & ODM Services** — Custom logo printing, custom colors, custom packaging, product modifications, and even custom product development from your designs.

**Global Shipping** — Sea freight (FCL/LCL), air freight, and express shipping (DHL, FedEx, UPS). We handle all export documentation and ship to 80+ countries.

## Our Service Process

1. **Inquiry** — Send us product interests via WhatsApp or Email
2. **Quotation** — Receive detailed pricing within 24 hours
3. **Sampling** — Order samples to verify quality (sample fee deductible from bulk order)
4. **Order Confirmation** — Confirm order details, MOQ, customization
5. **Production** — Production begins with regular quality updates
6. **Quality Control** — Pre-shipment inspection with photos/videos
7. **Shipping** — Arrange shipping with full export documentation
8. **After-Sales** — Dedicated account manager for ongoing support

## Quality Control Process

- Raw material inspection before production
- In-line quality checks during manufacturing
- Final random inspection (AQL 2.5) before packing
- Pre-shipment photos and videos for buyer confirmation
- Third-party testing available (SGS, Intertek, BV)
