## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**
A: MOQ varies by product, typically 50-500 pcs. We support mixed orders across different products within the same category. Contact us for specific product MOQ.

**Q: Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?**
A: Yes, we provide samples for quality verification. Sample fees are deductible from your bulk order. Sample lead time is 3-7 days.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union, and PayPal (for samples/small orders). Standard terms: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment.

**Q: Do you offer OEM/ODM services?**
A: Yes, we offer custom logo printing, custom colors, custom packaging, and full ODM product development from your designs. MOQ for customization varies.

**Q: What shipping methods are available?**
A: Sea freight (FCL/LCL from Ningbo or Shanghai), air freight, and express (DHL, FedEx, UPS, TNT). We handle all export documentation.

**Q: Do you provide quality test reports?**
A: Yes, we can provide CE, EN71, FDA test reports. Third-party testing (SGS, Intertek, BV) is available upon request.

**Q: How long is the production lead time?**
A: Standard lead time is 20-35 days depending on order quantity. Rush orders (15 days) are available with a surcharge.

**Q: Can you help with custom packaging?**
A: Yes, we offer custom retail packaging, gift boxes, display boxes, and bulk packaging. We can also help design your packaging.
