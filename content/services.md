## Quality Control

Our 4-step quality control process ensures you receive exactly what you ordered:

1. **Raw Material Inspection** — Verify material quality before production begins
2. **In-Line Quality Checks** — Regular inspections during the manufacturing process
3. **Final Random Inspection** — AQL 2.5 standard inspection before packing
4. **Pre-Shipment Verification** — Photos and videos sent for your final approval

Additional testing available:
- CE, EN71, FDA, CPC certification testing
- Third-party lab testing (SGS, Intertek, BV)
- Drop test, color fastness test, material composition analysis
